    What is semaglutide?

    A GLP-1 receptor agonist that slows how fast your stomach empties and quiets appetite signalling, so you feel full on less. Chime prescribes it as a compounded injection, dosed weekly and titrated by your clinician.

    What results can I expect?

    Published trials of injectable semaglutide report an average loss of around 15% of body weight over 68 weeks when paired with a reduced calorie diet and more activity. Results vary, it is not suitable for everyone, and your clinician decides whether it is appropriate for you. [CITATION TO CONFIRM]

    What is tirzepatide?

    A single weekly injection that acts on two gut hormone receptors, GIP and GLP-1, rather than one. For many people that means a stronger effect on appetite and on how the body handles sugar and fat.
