Chime Health offers compounded GLP-1s exclusively from U.S. pharmacies. Compounded medications are regulated and compounding pharmacies are licensed and inspected by Boards of Pharmacy, but the FDA has not evaluated the medications for safety, quality, or efficacy. GLP-1 medications are prescription drugs that should be taken and monitored under the supervision of a licensed health care professional. GLP-1 medications carry serious risks, including risks of thyroid C-cell tumors (which includes thyroid cancer) and medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC). Talk to your clinician if you get a lump or swelling in your neck, hoarseness, trouble swallowing, or shortness of breath.
