Frequently asked questions

Which pharmacies fill prescriptions for Chime members?

When a prescription is medically appropriate, it may be fulfilled by a licensed U.S. pharmacy partner.

Chime does not manufacture, compound, or dispense medications.

In which states is Chime available?

Provider availability and prescribing authority vary by state. A licensed provider in your state of residence reviews your medical intake and determines whether treatment is appropriate.

If treatment is not yet available where you live, you will be told during the assessment before anything is charged.

What happens after I complete my assessment?

Your next steps may include:

  • • Reviewing your recommended Chime path
  • • Viewing relevant program or pricing information
  • • Creating or accessing your Chime account
  • • Completing medical intake if you choose to continue
  • • Provider review
  • • Prescription treatment, if medically appropriate
  • • Fulfillment through a licensed U.S. pharmacy partner, when applicable
  • • Tracking your journey through your patient portal
  • • Accessing relevant membership and support resources

Your care team is available throughout at hello@chimehealth.com.

Do I need insurance?

No. Chime is a self-pay service — you do not need insurance to complete the assessment, see a provider, or receive treatment. Pricing is shown before you commit.

What should I know about GLP-1 medications?

Chime may provide access to provider-guided GLP-1 or GLP-1/GIP treatment options when medically appropriate. Completing the assessment does not guarantee a prescription; eligibility and treatment decisions are determined by a licensed provider after medical review.

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. The FDA does not evaluate compounded medications for safety, effectiveness, or quality in the same way it reviews FDA-approved drugs.

Your provider reviews your medical history, current medications, and contraindications (including a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2 syndrome) to determine whether treatment is appropriate for you.

What if I want to pause or cancel?

You and your provider decide together whether to continue at each refill. If you stop GLP-1 treatment, your provider may recommend a tapering schedule, and can discuss strategies for long-term weight management — weight regain after discontinuation is common.

Prescription products cannot be returned once shipped, so please review our Return & Refund Policy before ordering.

What is included in my membership?

Chime Membership is designed to be the ongoing relationship layer of the Chime experience. Depending on the program selected, membership may provide access to:

  • • Provider messaging
  • • Educational resources
  • • Progress and program tracking
  • • Medication tracking and refill support
  • • Personalized recommendations
  • • Patient portal resources
  • • Ongoing support

Specific membership benefits, pricing, and availability should be confirmed on the relevant membership or offer page.

What is the minimum age to enroll with Chime?

You must be at least 18 years of age (or the age of majority where you live) to complete the Chime Path Assessment™ and receive treatment through Chime. GLP-1 medications are not prescribed to anyone under 18 through this platform.

What happens if my BMI falls below 25?

GLP-1 treatment for weight management is generally indicated for obesity, or for overweight with a related condition. As your weight changes, your provider reassesses and may adjust your dose, move you to a maintenance plan, or pause treatment depending on your goals and risk factors.

Clinical decisions always remain with the licensed provider.
