Frequently asked questions

When a prescription is medically appropriate, it may be fulfilled by a licensed U.S. pharmacy partner. Chime does not manufacture, compound, or dispense medications.
The Chime Health membership is available to residents of all 50 U.S. states. Provider availability and prescribing authority vary by state. Your assigned provider will be licensed in your state of residence.
A licensed provider will review your health evaluation and conduct a telehealth consultation, which may take place via live video or through an asynchronous provider review, as determined by your provider and in accordance with the telehealth laws of your state. If the provider determines that treatment is appropriate, a prescription will be sent to a licensed pharmacy for fulfillment. Typical processing and delivery time is approximately 4–5 business days. Your care team is available throughout your membership at hello@chimehealth.com
No. The Chime Health membership is a self-pay healthcare service and does not require insurance. Flexible payment options are available.
GLP-1 receptor agonists are a class of medications originally developed for type 2 diabetes that have also been FDA approved (in their brand-name forms) for weight management in certain patients. The compounded versions available through the Chime Health membership are NOT FDA approved and have not been independently evaluated by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality. Your provider will evaluate your medical history, current medications, and contraindications (including a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2 syndrome) to determine whether treatment is appropriate for you.
You may pause or cancel your membership at any time. If you and your provider decide to discontinue GLP-1 treatment, your provider may recommend a tapering schedule. Weight regain after discontinuation is common and your provider can discuss strategies for long-term weight management.
Your Chime Health membership includes telehealth consultations with a licensed provider, a personalized care plan, prescription fulfillment if appropriate, and shipping. All pricing is displayed on our membership plans page.
You must be at least 18 years of age to enroll in the Chime Health membership. GLP-1 medications are not prescribed to individuals under 18 through this platform.
Because GLP-1s for weight management are indicated for obesity or overweight with a related condition, your clinician will reassess and may adjust dosing, shift to maintenance, or pause/discontinue depending on your health goals and risk factors.
