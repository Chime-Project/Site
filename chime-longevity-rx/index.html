Questions

Good to know

Can GLP-1 really affect how I age?

GLP-1 medications are studied primarily for metabolic and cardiovascular health. Because those systems are central to how the body ages, researchers are interested in their broader effects. Chime Health does not claim to slow, stop, or reverse aging; the protocol targets the metabolic markers your physician tracks. Individual results vary.

Why microdose for longevity specifically?

Longevity use means staying on a protocol for the long term rather than a short weight-loss cycle. Published dose-ranging data suggest tolerability may improve at lower doses, which is what makes a years-long protocol realistic. Your physician titrates to the lowest effective dose for you.

Is this the same medication as the brand-name pens?

The active medication is the same GLP-1 class (semaglutide or tirzepatide). The difference is the dose and the model: a physician-titrated microdose with monthly monitoring, built to run over time rather than the maximum dose.

Is it FDA-approved?

Compounded microdose GLP-1 is prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy and is not an FDA-approved finished drug product. A licensed physician evaluates your history and contraindications before any prescription is issued.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no long-term contract. You can cancel before your next billing cycle.
