How GLP-1 Works

GLP-1 is a naturally occurring hormone that supports the body's mechanisms for metabolic balance after meals.1

Studies examine GLP-1's potential role in:

  • Feeling lighter and more comfortable in daily movements2
  • Improved consistency with nutrition habits3
  • Greater confidence staying on track week to week4

How NAD+ Works

NAD+ is a natural coenzyme found in every cell of your body. It plays an important role in how cells turn nutrients into usable energy.5

As we age, NAD+ levels naturally decline.6

Studies examine NAD+'s potential role in:

  • Assisting in reducing the daily impact of stress7
  • Supporting mechanisms involved in promoting physical activity8, 9
  • Neuroprotection to stay on point10

Results may vary.
