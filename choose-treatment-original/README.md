# choose-treatment-original/ — Choose Your Treatment on the reference's exact price points

Client, 2026-10-04: "we have a version of this — https://intake.wellmedr.com/approval-confirmation — but can you rip this
with the exact price points etc". Luis: a new folder. Plan: `CHOOSE-TREATMENT-RIP-PLAN.md` §10 (untracked). Asana 1219139324256225.

| Page | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The `choose-treatment/` V1 rip brought up to the reference as it is on 2026-10-04: its own prices plus its two blue "12 months" upsell boxes |
| `checkout/` | `choose-treatment/checkout-v3/` on this folder's data: the reference's checkout figures for each of the 8 plans |

- Prices are the reference's, re-read from its page bundle and its checkout on 2026-10-04 (unchanged since the 2026-09-16
  rip). They are not stand-ins. Their `savingsToday` literals do not reconcile with their monthly price; kept verbatim.
- The upsell boxes: `upsell12: true` in `js/plans-data.js` turns them on in the shared renderer
  (`../choose-treatment/js/checkout.js`); the figure is the 12-month total − the 6-month total ($330 / $150), as theirs.
  `css/upsell.css` = the rules `../choose-treatment/css/checkout.css` lacked, copied from their sheet.
- Checkout, per plan (`checkout` blocks in the data, read by `../choose-treatment/checkout/js/plan-fill.js`): coupon 200off /
  250off / 500off applied on arrival, crossed-out = total + coupon, "Remove" = their no-coupon total, per day = monthly ÷ 30
  (no coupon: total ÷ days). Monthly plans: no coupon, "Standard Monthly Plan $X", any code is invalid, and the "By
  subscribing" disclaimer.
- Only the HTML, data, CSS and tests live here. V1's sheet, logo, vials and renderer, and the checkout's CSS, font, images
  and scripts, load from `../choose-treatment/`, so a fix there reaches these pages too.
- `checkout/index.html` is generated by untracked `uploads/wellmedoc-checkout-ref/build.py` section 6f; do not hand-edit.
- Carried from the reference and flagged in the head comment: the press strip, the 4.7 rating, the "chose this today"
  counters, the countdown, "Lowest industry pricing" and the other claims; the coupon, FSA/HSA, free benefits and reviews
  on the checkout. `noindex`.
- Tests: `node choose-treatment-original/js/original-tests.js` (89 checks: page figures, V1 parity, the two boxes and
  their placement, all 8 checkout summaries applied and removed, V2 rules unchanged).
